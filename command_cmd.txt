.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe main.py --course-dir "<path_to_course_dir>"


Example:
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe main.py --course-dir "C:\Users\user\Documents\Courses\2027\Semester A\LLM's"



Flag	Effect
--course-dir "../CourseName"	      auto-discover chapters from that folder's Lectures/Exercises subfolders (required); the final PDF is also copied there
--no-cache	                      re-call Claude for every chapter even if already cached
--refresh-context	              rebuild the full-course cross-reference digest
--with-exercises	              also summarize exercise/tutorial chapters (lectures only by default)
--with-tests	                      add a past-exam practice page after each chapter (needs an exams folder)
