.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe main.py --course-dir "<path_to_course_dir>"


Example:
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe main.py --course-dir "C:\Users\user\Documents\Courses\2027\Semester A\LLM's"



Flag	Effect
--course-dir "../CourseName"	      auto-discover chapters from that folder's Lectures/Exercises subfolders; the final PDF is also copied there
--no-cache	                      re-call Claude for every chapter even if already cached
--refresh-context	              rebuild the full-course cross-reference digest
--with-exercises	              also summarize exercise/tutorial chapters (lectures only by default)
--with-tests	                      add a past-exam practice page after each chapter (needs an exams folder)



Book mode (chapters of a textbook, shorter summary, optionally focused on an assignment):
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe main.py --book "<path_to_book.pdf>" --chapters <chapters> --focus "<path_to_assignment.pdf>"


Example:
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe main.py --book "C:\Users\user\Documents\Courses\2027\Semester A\LLM's\Speech_and_Language_Processing_2026.pdf" --chapters 1-5 --focus "C:\Users\user\Documents\Courses\2027\Semester A\LLM's\Assignments\ex1.pdf"



Flag	Effect
--book "book.pdf"	              summarize chapters of a textbook PDF (found from its bookmarks); the final PDF is also copied next to the book
--chapters 1-5	                      required with --book; chapter numbers, e.g. 1-5, 3, or 1,3-4
--focus "ex1.pdf"	              optional; explain in depth what this assignment relies on (never solves its questions)
--no-cache	                      also works with --book

Use exactly one of --course-dir or --book per run.
